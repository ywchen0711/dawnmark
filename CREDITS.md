# Credits

The Dawnmark (晨星之印) is an original game. Its story, characters, places, code and the
small world-map pictures are new. Everything else comes from artists who shared their work on
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org) under free licences. Thank you all.

Small monsters are drawn twice their size.
