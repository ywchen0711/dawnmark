# 晨星之印 The Dawnmark

An original 16-bit style RPG for the browser. A boy born with a star on his hand sets out to
light four lanterns, open the tower at the heart of the world, and stop the void that wants to
unpick every life. It plays in Chinese or English.

**Play:** https://ywchen0711.github.io/dawnmark/

## The story

Sixteen years ago a star fell on the mountain above Brightfall, and old Tobin found a baby in the
crater with a shining mark on its hand. On his sixteenth birthday Rowan climbs Lookout Crag with
his friend Wren, and the mark wakes.

He is sent to King Aurel, but the High Seer Morrow names him the Child of Ruin and has him thrown
into the dungeon. With Pell, the thief in the next cell, he escapes. Then the journey begins:

- Mirela, priestess of a drowned temple
- Odo, a monk who would rather cook
- Sera, a witch with no memory
- Brannoc, the knight sent to hunt them

Together they light the lanterns of water, fire, earth and frost, and climb the Skyloom. At the
top the truth waits, and the world goes dark. The second half of the game is about finding each
other again.

### The expansion

The journey is now much longer:
- **Gullwing Grotto**: before the boat is yours, win it back from Captain Rena and her goblin pirates.
- **Chimeholm and Galespire**: the Skyloom's door opens only to the Wind Bell, carried off by the storm roc Zephyra, in a new land across the eastern sea.
- **Mossmere and the Sunken Archive**: after the fall, learn the true name of High Seer Morrow and why he wanted to unpick the world. Call him by it in the last battle.
- **Each friend's own story**: Pell's mother in the Glimmer Mines, Mirela's drowned choir, Odo's master and his last recipe, Brannoc's brother the Black Lance, and Sera's lost childhood in the Thread Garden. Each one opens a fourth path on that friend's skill board.
- **After the end**: a second star falls into the sea. Explore the Starwell, face Eclipse, and, deeper still, the Thousand-Year Shade.
- Side stories: the lighthouse keeper's letters, the bards' old song of the first Dawnbearer, Gloomfen's hydra, Wren's shrine behind the falls.

Things to do besides the story:
- **The Crown Arena** in Luckhaven: six ranks, up to the champion Varro the Unbroken.
- **The casino**: slot reels and Dawn Poker, with prizes for coins.
- **Fishing** wherever the water ripples, for ten kinds of fish.
- **The forge**: the Snowveil smith makes gear from monster materials.
- **The Monster Book**, **Star Shards** for the collector and **six treasure maps** to dig up.
- A **journal** in the menu tells you what to do next.

Battles have more to them as well: about two hundred monsters, bosses that change shape and weaknesses as they are hurt, gather power, reflect spells or call for help, thunder, water and earth magic, ten more link arts, and accessories with effects such as regeneration, counters and extra experience.

## How it plays

Battles are seen from the front, in the old style.
- The monsters stand over a painted backdrop. Your party's windows run along the top, and the commands and messages sit at the bottom.
- Everyone acts in order of speed each round.
- A friend who takes a beating may become **fired up**. Fired-up friends can join in **link arts**.
- Friends other than Rowan can follow orders or be left to their own **tactics**.

Each friend has a **skill board**: three paths of skills and stat boosts, opened with the skill points earned at every level.

The rest is what you would expect:
- Towns with inns, shops and churches. Pray at a church to save. Fall in battle and you wake in the last church with half your gold.
- Glints on the ground and treasure chests.
- *Homeward* and the Homing Feather to fly back to towns.
- A boat, and later a sky drake.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows / WASD | Move |
| Z / Enter | Confirm and talk |
| X | Cancel; hold it to run |
| C / Tab | Menu |
| M | Music on or off |

Gamepads and touch screens work too.

## Running it

It is a static page with no build step. Serve the folder and open `index.html`, for example:

```
python -m http.server
```
